# SwapDesk — draft financial plan

Built in cfo.ai, 10 October 2026. USD; forecast October 2026–September 2028.

Proposed offer: $49/store/month includes 100 verified arranged exchanges; $0.50 per additional verified arrangement. All attempts incur cost. Merchant staff handle customer escalations. Pricing and demand are not validated.

- Main-Scenario: final monthly revenue $2,363; final operating profit ($588); final cash ($8,147); first negative cash Nov 2027.
- Growth: final monthly revenue $7,715; final operating profit $3,341; final cash $39,715; first negative cash None within horizon.
- Bad-Reliability: final monthly revenue $1,369; final operating profit ($1,678); final cash ($23,860); first negative cash Aug 2027.

All scenarios assume $15,000 opening cash (not observed funding), $0.03 per execution, and $2,000 monthly founder compensation from January 2027. Base merchant assumptions: 5 Small and 2 Growing opening stores, 1/0.5 new stores per month, 3%/2% churn, 120/400 attempted cases per store, 65% arrangement rate, 15% extra executions. Growth: 3/1.5 acquisition, 2%/1.5% churn, 75% arrangement rate, 10% extra executions. Bad Reliability: Base acquisition, 6%/5% churn, 45% arrangement rate, 60% extra executions and doubled technical-support time.

Other inputs: technical support 15/30 minutes/store at $25/hour; 90-minute onboarding; $100 acquisition spending/store; $75 fixed infrastructure + $1/store; $150 monthly overhead. No payroll burdens, tax, financing, capex or working-capital delays. Hackathon credits excluded from recurring economics.

All 24 months in three scenarios were reconciled for revenue, gross profit, operating profit and cash. A saved-model pricing driver test verified response without changing usage/payroll. Browser rendering checked light/dark themes, not slider gestures or clicks.

Limited reported outcome model-cost samples inform the allowance but do not validate production average costs or benchmark performance. See accompanying PDF for detailed assumptions and financial appendix; editable source is the saved cfo.ai model.
